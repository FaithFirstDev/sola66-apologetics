Install help

The apps are new and not yet signed with a developer certificate, so your computer or phone asks once whether you trust them. Here is what to tap.

Windows
  1. Download Sola66-Apologetics-Windows.zip, open it and choose Extract all.
  2. Open the extracted folder and double-click Install Sola66 Apologetics.
  3. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.
  4. Click Install. Tick Create a desktop shortcut if you’d like one. No administrator password is needed.
  5. Open Sola66 Apologetics from the Start menu.

To update, run a newer installer the same way; your library is kept. To uninstall, go to Settings › Apps › Installed apps.

Needs Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

Mac
  1. Download and open Sola66-Apologetics-Mac.dmg.
  2. Drag Sola66 Apologetics onto the Applications folder in the window that opens.
  3. Open Applications and double-click Sola66 Apologetics.
  4. macOS says it can’t verify the app. Click Done.
  5. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Sola66 Apologetics and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password or Touch ID. You only need to do this once.

If macOS asks whether the app may use your keychain, click Always Allow: the app keeps its security keys there. To update, drag the newer copy into Applications and choose Replace; your library is kept.

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or newer.

Android
  1. On your phone, tap Download for Android. If your browser warns that the file could be harmful, choose Download anyway.
  2. Tap the downloaded file (in the notification, or in Files › Downloads).
  3. If Android asks, allow installing apps from this source: tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect says the app is unrecognized, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  5. Open Sola66 Apologetics, then open Sync on your computer and on the phone and scan the code to bring your library over.

To update, install a newer copy the same way; your notes and favorites are kept.

Needs Android 14 or newer.
